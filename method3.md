V2 Geodesic dome with simple instructions (Method 3)

We have 2 lengths for our spans (90mm and 106mm)
You will construct the dome from the top down
The dome in constructed of only 2 sizes/shapes of triangles
The dome has no "dents" (reverse folds) in it.
The dome has no holes or empty parts.

1. Construct the top level by taking 5 short spans and connecting in a start from a 5 slot hub

2. Join the other end (using 5 x 6 slot hubs) of the 5 spans together with 5 long spans so that we have a pentagonal star. 

   CRITICAL 3D GEOMETRY: The pentagonal star cannot lie flat due to the edge length constraints. The 5 outer vertices must form a regular pentagon where:
   - Pentagon radius R = long_span / (2 × sin(36°)) = 106mm / (2 × sin(36°)) ≈ 90.1mm
   - The apex height above this pentagon is calculated using Pythagorean theorem:
     h² + R² = short_span²
     h² + (90.1)² = (90)²
     h² = 8100 - 8118 = -18 (This shows 90mm/106mm spans don't work for a perfect V2!)
   
   For the physical construction example (124mm/140mm spans):
   - Pentagon radius R = 140mm / (2 × sin(36°)) ≈ 119.0mm  
   - Apex height h = √(124² - 119²) ≈ 34.9mm
   
   The key insight: The pentagon vertices are positioned by their mutual distances (long spans), then the apex height emerges from the spoke constraint (short spans).

3. using 10 long spans make 5 triangles OUTWARD from outer edge of the pentagonal star. Use 5 x 6 slot hubs to make the outer vertex of each triangle

4. In the remaining slot of each pentagonal star vertex add 5 short spans radially outwards

5. Join the edges of the triangles to the adjacent new short spans with alternating 5 slot hubs and 6 slot hubs.

6. Pull up by the center of the pentagonal star to start to form a dome. We now have 3 levels of vertices (apex, outer edge of pentagonal star, middle level)

7. To start to form the base level, now connect into the open slots of our hubs. You will put 2 long spans into each 6 slot hub and 2 short spans into each 5 slot hub

8. Join each long span to its adjacent short span using 6 slot hubs

9. Now to finish the base layer, using 10 long spans, and 10 x 4 slot hubs,  join all the other adjacent span ends while pulling up from the center of the pentagonal star. You now have a V2 geodesic dome!

10. For clarity: The two triangle types are: 
  - The top triangles are (short, short, long - per Steps 1&2. The shorts are both  connected to the apex vertex)
  - The middle layer/level is alternately (long, long, long - per Steps 2&3) AND (short, short, long - per Steps 4&5), 
  - the bottom layer/level is alternately (long, long, long) AND (short, short, long).
  - 40 triangles in total. Top: 5 triangles, Middle: 15 triangles, Bottom: 20 triangles. There are ZERO "right-angle triangles".
  - 26 vertices in total. Apex: 1, Top Pentagon: 5 vertexes, Middle: 10 vertexes, Bottom: 10 vertexes.
  - If each triangle was solid, then there are ZERO gaps (empty shapes) in the final dome structure.
